# Task 1 - Digital Literacy Infographic

## Tool Used
Canva (canva.com)

## What the Design Shows
The infographic is titled "Digital Literacy: Your Superpower in the Digital Age" and covers four key topics:
1. **What Digital Literacy Is** - the ability to find, evaluate, and communicate information using digital technology
2. **Useful Digital Tools for Students** - Google Workspace, Notion, Grammarly, Canva, Zotero
3. **Safe Internet Practices** - strong passwords, two-factor authentication, recognizing phishing
4. **Email Etiquette** - clear subject lines, professional tone, timely replies

## Reflection
One thing I found interesting while making this infographic was balancing visual appeal with information density. Fitting meaningful content into a single page without making it feel cluttered was a real design challenge. Using icons and color-coded sections helped organize the information clearly and made the infographic easy to read at a glance.
