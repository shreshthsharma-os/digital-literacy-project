# Case Study: UPI Payment Fraud

## Type of Cybercrime
UPI / Online Payment Fraud (a form of financial phishing)

## What the Crime Is
UPI Payment Fraud is a type of cybercrime where attackers trick victims into
authorizing payments by exploiting their unfamiliarity with UPI (Unified Payments
Interface) apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm. Unlike traditional hacking,
this crime requires no technical skills - it relies entirely on social engineering.


## How It Typically Happens - Step by Step

1. The attacker posts a fake advertisement online - selling a second-hand laptop,
   offering a work-from-home job, or posing as a buyer on OLX/Quikr.
2. A college student responds, interested in the deal or opportunity.
3. The attacker builds trust through friendly, convincing conversation over WhatsApp
   or Telegram, sometimes over several days.
4. The attacker sends a "collect/request money" on a UPI app, which looks nearly
   identical to a "send money" transaction on screen.
5. The victim, confused by the interface, enters their UPI PIN - believing they are
   receiving money, not authorizing a payment.
6. The money is instantly and irreversibly debited from the victim's account.
7. The attacker immediately blocks the victim and disappears.


## Who Is Usually Targeted
College students and young adults are prime targets because they are new to digital
payments, often trust online strangers, and may not read UPI prompts carefully.
First-time job seekers, hostel residents selling used goods, and people responding
to "easy money" opportunities are especially vulnerable.


## Consequences
- Immediate financial loss (commonly ₹5,000 - ₹50,000)
- Psychological distress, embarrassment, and loss of trust
- Extreme difficulty recovering funds, as UPI transactions are near-instant and
  often irreversible
- In some cases, victims are manipulated into multiple transactions before realising


## Source
Based on patterns documented by the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
(cybercrime.gov.in) and widely reported by Indian financial news outlets (2022-2024).
