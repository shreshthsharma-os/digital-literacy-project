# Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist for College Students

## General Online Safety

1. Use strong, unique passwords for every account - use a password manager
   (like Bitwarden or Google Password Manager) if needed.
2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all important accounts
   (email, banking, GitHub, LinkedIn).
3. Never click on links in unsolicited emails or SMS messages -
   go directly to the official website instead.
4. Keep your phone's OS and apps updated - updates often patch critical
   security vulnerabilities.
5. Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking or logging into sensitive accounts.
   Use a VPN if necessary.
6. Do not download apps or software from unofficial sources or unknown websites.

## 💸 UPI & Financial Safety (Critical)

7. Always read the UPI prompt carefully before entering your PIN.
   "Enter PIN to PAY" means money is LEAVING your account.
   You NEVER need to enter your PIN to RECEIVE money.

8. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank CVV with anyone -
   not even someone claiming to be a bank employee or customer support agent.

## Social & Identity Safety

9. Set your social media profiles to private and regularly review your
   follower and connection lists.
10. Never post photos of your college ID, Aadhaar card, PAN card, or any
    government document on any platform.

## 🚨 If You Are a Victim - Report Immediately

- 🌐 **Online:** https://cybercrime.gov.in
- 📞 **Helpline:** Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline - available 24x7)
- 🏦 **Bank:** Inform your bank within 24 hours to attempt a transaction reversal

> Reporting quickly increases the chance of recovering lost funds.
> Do not feel embarrassed - these crimes are designed to fool anyone.
