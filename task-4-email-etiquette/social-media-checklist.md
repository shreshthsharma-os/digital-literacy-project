# Social Media Do's and Don'ts for College Students

## DO's

1. Keep your LinkedIn profile updated with your education, skills, and projects.
2. Use a professional profile photo on platforms like LinkedIn and GitHub.
3. Think before you post - ask yourself if you'd be comfortable with a recruiter seeing it.
4. Follow industry leaders, professors, and companies relevant to your field.
5. Credit others when sharing or reposting their work or ideas.
6. Use privacy settings to control who sees your personal posts.
7. Engage positively - comment thoughtfully on professional discussions.

## DON'Ts

1. Don't share personal information like your phone number, address, or ID publicly.
2. Don't post offensive, discriminatory, or politically inflammatory content.
3. Don't badmouth professors, classmates, or institutions online.
4. Don't use social media to spread unverified news or rumours.
5. Don't accept friend/connection requests from unknown people without verifying.
6. Don't share exam papers, answer keys, or copyrighted academic material online.
7. Don't overshare your daily routine - it can be a safety and privacy risk.

---

> Remember: Your digital footprint is permanent. What you post today can affect
> your career and reputation years from now. Think twice, post once.
